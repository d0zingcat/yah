Yah contextual candidate ranking, Chinese word vectors

Based on “Learning Word Vectors for 157 Languages” (2018), by Edouard Grave,
Piotr Bojanowski, Prakhar Gupta, Armand Joulin and Tomas Mikolov.
https://aclanthology.org/L18-1550/
https://fasttext.cc/docs/en/crawl-vectors.html
Original Common Crawl and Wikipedia vectors:
https://dl.fbaipublicfiles.com/fasttext/vectors-crawl/cc.zh.300.vec.gz

Yah selects the first 48,000 two-to-four Han character terms in original
frequency order, folds the 300 dimensions into 64 using a fixed signed
projection and quantizes each vector independently to signed 8-bit values.
The conversion is reproducible using Tools/ContextVectors/rebuild.py.
